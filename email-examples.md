## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Before drafting an email, scan for:
1. **An example to a similar person/role** — use as a structural template
2. **Subject line patterns that worked** — especially starred examples
3. **P.S. constructions** — see what gets numbered vs. single-line
4. **Proof point phrasing** — how stats are introduced, how bolding is applied

The highest-signal examples are the ones marked ✅ (sent, got a reply/meeting).

- "Starting to crack" not "cracked" — calibrated confidence for a senior buyer who owns the P&L.
- Single-item P.S. with a *timely* hook (Knicks in the finals, last night's loss) instead of 3 numbered items. Timeliness > quantity when the news hook is fresh.

- Credibility block rendered as 2 bullets (clients + UK gov case study with links) rather than one prose sentence.

## FORMATTING REMINDER

Per playbook voice rules: bold should be on **proof points** (stats, framework names, specific claims), NOT on bullet headers.

In sent emails that booked meetings:
- Bold: **BCG pilot**, **49% to 86%**, **1,400+ artifacts**, **AI Fluency Index**
- Don't bold: "Measurement." "Gap." "Business impact."
