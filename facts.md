3. **Same school** — Penn, anyone teaching at Penn GSE
4. **Same career path** — banking to tech, education to consulting, founder to fractional
5. **Same framework** — AIQ vs. 4D, BCG ROLI, 10-20-70, Skills England
6. **Same client** — BCG, Vanta, EY, UCLA, Anthropic, anyone in your client logo set
7. **Same essay/post** — "2024 EdTech venture studios," AIQ x 4D crosswalk, JFF publication

1. **Tier 1 — Transformation stats** (verb + time-bound + dramatic): "49% to 86% AI power users in 3 weeks"; "7% to 75% fluent with AI in 3 weeks" (BCG, fluency framing — used with Pearson/Melissa Matlins June 2026)
